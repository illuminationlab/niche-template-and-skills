## Long description
> LimoBooked is booking-first software for limousine and livery operators. Unlike dispatch-only tools, it starts where the revenue does: a 24/7 AI receptionist answers every call, quotes your services, and books the ride — or texts a booking link before the caller hangs up — so leads that used to hit voicemail and call a competitor land on your calendar instead. It bundles online booking, dispatch, a CRM, two-way texting, automated quote follow-up, missed-call text-back, reviews/reputation, invoicing, and marketing automation into one platform. Pricing is a single flat $99/month with everything included (first 270 operators lock that rate forever), plus transparent pay-as-you-go for the texts, calls, and AI minutes you actually use — no per-seat fees, no hidden tiers, no annual contract.
